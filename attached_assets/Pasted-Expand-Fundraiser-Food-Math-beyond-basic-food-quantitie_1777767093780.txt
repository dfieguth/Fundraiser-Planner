Expand Fundraiser Food Math beyond basic food quantities into a more complete Full Event Pack.

Goal:
Make the paid Full Event Pack feel like a complete fundraiser execution plan, not just a shopping calculator.

Do not redesign the whole app.
Do not change Stripe links.
Do not change unlock logic.
Do not add accounts, database, AI, or external APIs.

Add these sections to the full unlocked results and print page:

1. Fundraiser Strategy Summary
Generate a short summary based on the event:
- Best fit for this meal type
- Main profit driver
- Main execution risk
- Recommended focus for the leader

Example:
“For this size event, your main risk is serving speed. Keep the line simple, stage food before guests arrive, and assign Student Runners to restock supplies.”

2. Profit Strategy
Add practical recommendations:
- Suggested donation/meal price check
- Upsell ideas based on meal type
- Donation table recommendation
- Suggested signage language
- Whether to use fixed price, suggested donation, or donation basket

Examples:
- “Add drinks or dessert as a simple donation upsell.”
- “Use a visible donation station near the entrance/exit.”
- “For church events, suggested donation may feel more natural than a hard price.”

3. Volunteer Briefing
Generate a short copyable briefing the leader can read to volunteers before the event.

Include:
- Goal of the event
- Serving flow
- Food safety reminder
- Who handles restocking
- Who handles cleanup
- Who handles donations/payment

4. Setup Layout Guidance
Add a simple serving-line layout recommendation based on meal type:
- Start of line
- Main food station
- Toppings/condiments
- Drinks
- Donation/payment station
- Trash/cleanup area

Use text-based guidance only. No diagram needed yet.

5. Timeline With Leader Notes
Improve the prep timeline so each time block includes:
- Task
- Owner/team
- Leader note
- Watch-out

6. Leftover Plan
Add a short leftover guidance section:
- What can be saved
- What should be discarded
- How to package leftovers
- Who decides what to do with extras

Keep food safety language basic and cautious.

7. Parent/Student Communication Pack
Add copyable text for:
- Parent/student announcement
- Volunteer request message
- Day-before reminder
- Thank-you message after the fundraiser

Use warm, practical wording. Avoid sounding overly corporate.

8. Risk Plan
For each risk warning, add a practical fix.

Example:
Warning: “Low adult volunteer count.”
Fix: “Assign adults to cooking, money handling, and oversight. Use students for running, restocking, and cleanup.”

9. Shopping List Improvements
Add grouping by category:
- Protein/main items
- Bread/grains/tortillas
- Dairy
- Produce
- Toppings/condiments
- Drinks
- Paper goods
- Kitchen/service supplies

Do not add live store pricing.

10. Maintain trust language
Include:
“These are planning estimates. Adjust for your group, appetite, store prices, and local context.”

- Full unlocked results show the new sections
- Print page includes the new sections cleanly
- Free preview does not give away the full event pack
- Mobile layout still works
- Type check passes
