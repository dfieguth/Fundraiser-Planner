Update all serving size calculations across every meal type using research-backed portion data. Apply these verified serving sizes as the foundation for all quantity calculations.

CORE PHILOSOPHY TO APPLY EVERYWHERE:

1. Buffet premium: Add 10% to all primary proteins and starches because self-serve buffets consistently result in guests taking larger portions than sit-down meals.

2. Multiple option rule: When two meal types are offered together, total quantity only needs to increase by 30% not double. Guests naturally take smaller portions of each when multiple options exist.

3. Kids adjustment: Children eat approximately 60% of an adult portion for proteins and starches. Always apply this when audience mix includes kids.

4. Never double-count: Condiments and toppings are shared across guests who choose them. Never calculate as if every guest uses every topping at full serving size.

- Buns: match hot dog count exactly plus 5% buffer
