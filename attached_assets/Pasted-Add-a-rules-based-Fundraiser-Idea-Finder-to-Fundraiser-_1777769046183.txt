Add a rules-based Fundraiser Idea Finder to Fundraiser Food Math.

Goal:
Help users who do not know what food fundraiser to run choose a good option, then pre-fill the existing planner.

Important:
This should be a simple rules-based recommendation wizard, not an AI agent.
Do not add OpenAI API, external APIs, maps, live store pricing, or location lookup.

Add a new page/route:
- /idea-finder

Add entry points:
- Landing page secondary CTA: “Help Me Choose a Fundraiser”
- Planner page small link near the sample templates: “Not sure what to serve? Try the Idea Finder”

Quiz fields:
1. Organization type:
Church, School, Sports Team, Nonprofit, Other

2. Expected attendance:
Under 75, 75–150, 150–250, 250+

3. Audience:
Mostly kids/students, Mostly adults, Mixed

4. Time of event:
Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, After church/school, During a larger event

5. Adult volunteer count:
0–2, 3–5, 6–10, 10+

6. Student/helper count:
0–3, 4–8, 9–15, 16+

7. Equipment available:
Kitchen, Oven, Grill, Large pots, Warmers/roasters, Refrigerator/cooler space, No kitchen

8. Priorities:
Lowest cost, Easiest execution, Highest profit potential, Fastest serving line, Kid-friendly, Adult-friendly

9. Constraints:
No cooking onsite, Limited prep time, Limited adult supervision, Need grab-and-go, Need vegetarian option

Recommendation logic:
Recommend from existing meal types:
- Hot Dogs
- Burgers
- Baked Potatoes
- Breakfast Burritos
- Tacos
- Spaghetti
- Pancakes

For each recommendation, show:
- Rank: Best Overall, Easiest, Highest Profit, Best Fit, etc.
- Meal type
- Why this fits
- Difficulty: Easy, Moderate, High
- Expected profit potential: Low, Medium, High
- Volunteer need: Low, Medium, High
- Equipment needed
- Watch-outs
- Button: “Build This Plan”

When the user clicks “Build This Plan”:
- Pre-fill the existing planner with realistic default values based on the quiz
- User can edit everything before generating results

Rules guidance:
- Hot Dogs: best for low volunteers, kids, fast serving, low equipment
- Burgers: good for high attendance with grill access and enough adult volunteers
- Baked Potatoes: good profit, adult-friendly, oven/warmers helpful, slower prep
- Breakfast Burritos: best for breakfast/grab-and-go, requires prep and foil wrapping
- Tacos: good for youth/groups, moderate prep, needs serving line organization
- Spaghetti: good for large dinner events, needs kitchen/large pots, adult volunteers
- Pancakes: good for breakfast, low ingredient cost, needs griddle/cooking team
